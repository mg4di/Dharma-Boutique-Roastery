# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vue 3, Vite, Vue Router, Pinia, vue-i18n. No backend; demo and visitor data stays in localStorage.

## Users

Indonesian coffee drinkers browsing on desktop or mobile who want to discover and buy locally roasted Indonesian coffee.

## Product Purpose

Dharma Boutique Roastery is a frontend storefront and brand experience for Indonesian local coffee. Success means visitors can discover a coffee that suits their taste and brew method, understand its origin, and complete a simulated order.

## Positioning

Kopi Lokal & Roastery. Origin and flavor discovery should make Indonesian coffee easier to understand and choose than a generic bean catalog.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse local coffee origins, compare roast and flavor, select bean size and grind, and use a frontend-only cart and simulated checkout. Indonesian is the default language; English is also supported.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Vue frontend only. No server, real payments, account system, cross-device sync, or real order fulfillment. Cart, wishlist, orders, and other demo data use localStorage. Product names, prices, farmer and origin details, addresses, and imagery are placeholder content for the prototype and must not be presented as verified business facts.

## Brand Commitments

Business name: Dharma Boutique Roastery. Business descriptor: Kopi Lokal & Roastery. Indonesian-first content. Warm, craft-focused storytelling about Indonesian local coffee.

## Evidence on Hand

The project PRD provides sample coffee origins, a placeholder catalog and prices, and a photo direction. No verified business assets, testimonials, farmer partnerships, address, or product inventory are supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make coffee discovery approachable through flavor, origin, and brew guidance.
- Treat Indonesian coffee origins as useful product information, not decoration.
- Keep shopping interactions clear and complete on mobile.
- Label frontend-only behavior and placeholder content honestly.
- Keep the experience functional without a backend.
